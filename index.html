Life Happens, Coffee Helps

Born out of a deep reverence for traditional brewing techniques, Swarnashrunga Filter Coffee Decoction is crafted to bring you the nostalgic, aromatic essence of pure, full-bodied Kaapi without the wait.

We source high-grade Arabica and Robusta beans directly from sun-drenched estates, roasting and grinding them to perfection before slowly brewing a thick, flavorful decoction. Free of artificial preservatives, flavors, or additives, every drop captures the ideal balance of strength, smoothness, and lingering aroma.

We select AA-quality seeds for our coffee decoction preparation, which is premium and export quality, manufactured in natural processes as per norms of the central government food research institute.
