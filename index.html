Our Story

Born from a Deep Reverence
for Tradition

At Swarnashrunga, we believe that the true soul of South India lies in a fresh, rich cup of authentic filter coffee. Born out of a deep reverence for traditional brewing techniques, Swarnashrunga Filter Coffee Decoction is crafted to bring you the nostalgic, aromatic essence of pure, full-bodied Kaapi — without the wait.

We source high-grade Arabica and Robusta beans directly from sun-drenched estates, roasting and grinding them to perfection before slowly brewing a thick, flavorful decoction. Every drop captures the ideal balance of strength, smoothness, and lingering aroma — making it effortless to pour, mix with warm milk, and savor the comforting ritual of classic South Indian coffee wherever you are.

"Coffee is nature's gift. Nobody can manufacture taste. Taste is incorporated within the seeds by nature."

The flavor and aroma of the coffee depends on how we select and blend these seeds. We select AA quality of seeds for our coffee decoction preparation, which is premium and export quality. We manufacture filter coffee decoction in natural processes as per norms of the central government food research institute.

Crafted for the Perfect Cup

An important factor in great coffee is the Coffee & Chicory blend. We use 80% coffee to 20% chicory — a very standard and time-tested procedure that delivers unmatched depth.

Within the 80% coffee component, we blend 60% Arabica with 20% Robusta & Peaberry. This proportion is very standard and rarely available in the market — equivalent to top coffee brands.

There are different qualities ranging from AAA to C available in the market. We select AA quality — premium and export grade — for our decoction preparation.

Preparation Guide

Now Prepare Premium Restaurant-Type
Coffee at Home

01

Measure

Take 10–15 ml of Swarnashrunga decoction for a strong brew, or 8 ml for a medium cup.

02

Heat Milk

Boil 80–100 ml of fresh milk until frothy. The froth is essential for that authentic filter coffee experience.

03

Combine

Pour the decoction into your tumbler, add the hot frothy milk, and sweeten with 1–2 teaspoons of sugar to taste.

04

Savor

Mix well by pouring between a tumbler and davara. Enjoy the perfect cup of South Indian filter coffee!
