Crafted for the Authentic Cup

An essential factor in legendary South Indian filter coffee is the exact coffee-to-chicory ratio. We use 80% pure coffee and 20% chicory — a time-tested formulation perfected over generations.

Within our 80% coffee core, we blend 60% high-elevation Arabica with 20% select Robusta & Peaberry. This proportion creates an unmatched balance rarely found in mass commercial brands.

Simple Recipe Guide

Prepare Restaurant-Style Filter Coffee at Home

No brass filter setup required. Enjoy hot frothy Kaapi in 3 quick steps.

01

Measure Decoction

Pour 10 to 15 ml of Swarnashrunga decoction into your coffee tumbler or davara.

02

Heat Frothy Milk

Boil 80 to 100 ml of full-cream fresh milk until piping hot and frothy.

03

Combine & Pour

Pour the hot milk over decoction, add sugar to taste, and froth between tumbler & davara!

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of Swarnashrunga Filter Coffee Decoction?

Swarnashrunga decoction has a shelf life of 35 days at room temperature when unopened. Once opened, keep it refrigerated and consume within 15–20 days for maximum aroma and flavor.

Do I need to boil the decoction before mixing?

No, there is no need to boil the decoction itself! Simply pour 10–15 ml of decoction into your cup and pour boiling, frothy hot milk directly over it.

Are there any artificial preservatives or added sugars?

None at all! Swarnashrunga decoction is 100% natural, containing only 80% AA grade coffee and 20% premium chicory brewed in pure water according to Central Govt food research norms.
